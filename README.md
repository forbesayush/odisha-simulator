## 🎭 The 7-Act Dramatic Screenplay & Showdowns

1. **Act I: The Rasulgarh Flyover Gridlock (9:15 AM)**
   - *Opponent:* **Bunty "Neon Pulsar" Jena** (Wrong-side Divider Specialist)
   - *Confrontation:* *"Dekhiki chala be! Rasta ta tama bapa ra ki?"*
   - *Audio Trigger:* Raspy KTM throttle revs & Mo Bus Route 20 multi-tone air horn.

2. **Act II: The Sonic Call of the Dahibara Seller (10:30 AM)**
   - *Opponent:* **Siddharth "AirPods" Mohapatra** (Ketchup & Sweet Corn Terrorist)
   - *Setting:* Saheed Nagar ancient banyan tree, Bidyadhar Mausa's 40-year aluminum Handi.
   - *Audio Trigger:* *THAK-THAK-THUNG! ... Kudi-Kudi-THUNG!* Metallic ladle bell harmonics.

3. **Act III: The "Bachelor Hunting" Student Rent Scam (1:15 PM)**
   - *Opponents:* **Landlord Prafulla Uncle & Ramesh Broker**
   - *Setting:* Deep Patia waterlogged lane, neon-green G+2 building.
   - *Demands:* ₹500 non-refundable visiting fee, ₹13/unit submeter, 9 PM gate padlock, strictly family only.

4. **Act IV: The Omfed "Tookna" & Pan Dukan Khatti (5:30 PM)**
   - *Opponent:* **Tookna Bhai** (Master of the ₹2 Melody Chocolate Change)
   - *Setting:* Classic green-and-white Omfed booth, twilight geopolitical debates.
   - *Audio Trigger:* *PHAT-PHAT-PHAT! ... CHHAAK!* Acoustic betel leaf chuna palm slap.

5. **Act V: The Fake "IT Placement / Backdoor Offer" Scam (7:00 PM)**
   - *Opponent:* **HR Director Subrat** (MS-Paint 6.5 LPA Bangalore Package Architect)
   - *Setting:* 10x10 cabin near Jaydev Vihar, leaking split-AC dripping into bucket.
   - *Showdown:* Expose the fake offer letter with LeetCode Hard and GSTIN queries.

6. **Act VI: The Kalabaisakhi Cyclone Panic at Unit-1 Haat (8:30 PM)**
   - *Opponents:* **Barabati Aloo Syndicate & Sambalpuri Saree Aunties**
   - *Setting:* Bruised purple skies, frantic WhatsApp Kanak News forwards, ₹60/kg potato surge.
   - *Audio Trigger:* Low-frequency Kalabaisakhi thunder rumble & gusting cyclonic winds.

7. **Act VII: Final Showdown at Master Canteen (10:30 PM)**
   - *Final Boss:* **Pink Vikram Auto Anna** (Emperor of the ₹350 Kalpana Square Surge)
   - *Setting:* Master Canteen stone warrior horse in the pouring rain.
   - *Showdown:* Deploy everything in your Jhola—from 5kg potatoes to Tookna's chocolates—to achieve Twin-City enlightenment.

## 🔊 100% Client-Side Web Audio Synthesizer

Zero external MP3s or audio asset dependencies. All sounds are mathematically generated on the fly via the browser's Web Audio API:
- **Dahibara Ladle Strike:** FM-synthesized resonant aluminum bell harmonics.
- **Tookna Betel Slap:** Dual-layer white noise burst with rapid transient exponential envelope.
- **Vikram Auto Horn & Mo Bus:** Sawtooth dual-tone oscillator array.
- **Kalabaisakhi Thunder:** Low-pass filtered pink noise with 40Hz sub-bass decay.
- **8-Bit Combat SFX:** Retro arcade hit blips, crits, and victory jingles.

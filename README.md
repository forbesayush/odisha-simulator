## 🌟 What is Bhubaneswar 3D?

Inspired by **[FightKaro.com](https://fightkaro.com)**, **[Benglooru.com](https://benglooru.com)**, and **[DelhiMetroGame.com](https://delhimetrogame.com)**, **Bhubaneswar 3D: Ama Gadi, Ama Rasta** is a full 3D browser-based open-world experience combining hyper-local Odia urban satire, vehicular free-roam, arcade brawls, and groundbreaking **Antigravity & Emergent Physics Sandbox mechanics**.

Cruise down **Janpath** on a nimble **White Activa 6G** or the heavy **1998 Pink Vikram Auto**, dodge green **Mo Buses** and sacred cows, engage in street brawls outside **Saheed Nagar Dahibara** and **Omfed Tookna Khatti**, flip gravity to fly 40 meters into the sky over flyovers, and unleash electromagnetic **Repulsor Pulses** that launch street pots, potato sacks, and police barricades into weightless zero-G orbit!

## 🛸 Core Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Antigravity Mechanics (ଶୂନ୍ୟ ମାଧ୍ୟାକର୍ଷଣ)
- **Zero-G Toggle (`[G]`):** Invert or neutralize gravity at the tap of a button. Glowing cyan/magenta plasma repulsor fields ignite beneath vehicle wheels.
- **Vertical Thruster Flight:** Press `Space` to engage upward repulsor thrusters (hover up to 42m above Janpath), and `Shift` / `C` to dive back down.
- **Rooftop & Flyover Cruising:** Fly above Rasulgarh flyovers, land on high-rise helipads, and glide over gridlocked city traffic.
- **Electromagnetic Shockwave Pulse (`[Q]` / `[B]`):** Unleashes an omnidirectional repulsor shockwave, hurling all movable physics debris within a 35m radius into high-speed zero-G orbit!

### 2. Emergent Physics Sandbox (ଭୌତିକ ବିଜ୍ଞାନ ଜୁଗାଡ଼)
- **30+ Dynamic Rigid Bodies:** Aluminum Dahibara Handis (ବାସନ), 50kg Potato/Rice Sacks (ବସ୍ତା), Yellow Police Barricades, Tookna wooden crates, coconut heaps, and flying Kalabaisakhi tin roofs.
- **Complex Multi-Body Interactions:** Objects possess mass, velocity, restitution, and rotational momentum. Ramming into a sack rolls it into traffic; bumping a handi clatters it against barricades; triggering a pulse sets off chaotic cascading chain reactions.
- **Weightless Cow Levitation:** Nearby cows caught in the pulse gently float upside-down in zero-G with comedic Odia charm.

### 3. Benglooru.com-Style Urban Satire & Survival Stats
- **Pakhala Stamina (`ପଖାଳ ଷ୍ଟାମିନା`):** Keeps you energized under the hot coastal sun.
- **Street Jugaad (`ଭାଇଚାରା ଜୁଗାଡ଼`):** Social leverage to convince auto walas, landlords, and tea stalls.
- **Karma Meter (`ଶାନ୍ତି`):** Tracks your peace of mind amidst traffic madness.
- **Wallet (`ଟଙ୍କା`):** Manage cash for ₹40 Dahibara plates, ₹25 Tookna paan, or ₹150 pink auto fares.
- **Live Weather API Integration:** Automatically fetches real-time temperature and humidity directly from Bhubaneswar weather sensors via Open-Meteo REST API, with a manual toggle for the cyclonic **Kalabaisakhi thunderstorm** (3D rain particles, lightning, dark skies).

### 4. FightKaro.com 3D Street Brawls
- Approach any landmark hotspot and press `[E]` to trigger interactive dialogue trees or initiate a 1v1 street brawl.
- Dynamic side-view fighting arena camera framing combatants in real-time.
- Localized combat moves with visual 3D knockback:
  - 🥣 **Guguni Splash** (28 DMG)
  - 🖐️ **Tookna Chuna Slap** (34 DMG)
  - 🍯 **Pahala Rasagola Slam** (45 DMG)
  - 👥 **Vani Vihar Squad** (40 DMG)

## 🌐 Trilingual Localization

Easily toggle between three languages on the fly:
- **Odia (`ଓଡ଼ିଆ`):** Authentic vernacular with Noto Sans Oriya typography (`ଶୂନ୍ୟ ମାଧ୍ୟାକର୍ଷଣ`, `ପଖାଳ ଷ୍ଟାମିନା`, `ଭାଇଚାରା ଜୁଗାଡ଼`, `ବାସନ ଉଡ଼ୁଛି!`).
- **Hindi (`हिंदी`):** Hindi urban street adaptation (`एंटी-ग्रेविटी मोड`, `पखाल स्टेमिना`, `जुगाड़ लेवल`, `बर्तन उड़ रहे हैं!`).
- **English:** Clean retro arcade HUD strings and internationalized indicators.

## 🔊 100% Procedural Web Audio Engine

No `.mp3` or `.wav` files to download. All audio is mathematically synthesized at runtime:
- **Antigravity Thruster Jet:** Frequency-swept sine wave with resonant band-pass filtering.
- **Electromagnetic Shockwave:** Booming sub-bass drop ($500\text{Hz} \to 45\text{Hz}$) with high sci-fi chime.
- **Dahibara Aluminum Ladle Clang:** High resonant bell harmonics ($940\text{Hz}$, $980\text{Hz}$, $540\text{Hz}$).
- **Tookna Betel Lime Slap:** Dual-layer white noise burst with rapid exponential decay.
- **Vehicle Horns:** Dual harmonic sawtooth ($360\text{Hz}$ for Vikram Auto) and square wave ($520\text{Hz}$ for Activa).
